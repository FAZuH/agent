**CRITICAL OUTPUT RULES:**
- Output ONLY the transcribed text.
- Do NOT include introductory text (e.g., "Here is the transcription:").
- Do NOT include explanations or commentary about the handwriting.
- Do NOT include closing remarks or summaries.
- Output the transcription directly and nothing else.

**TRANSCRIPTION GUIDELINES:**
- Transcribe the text exactly as written, preserving spelling and punctuation.
- Maintain line breaks and paragraph structure where discernible.
- Preserve the original spacing between words and lines as much as possible.
- If the handwriting is unclear, make your best interpretation without adding uncertainty notes.
- Preserve capitalization as it appears in the handwriting.
- If multiple sections or notes are present, transcribe them in the order they appear.
- If the handwriting contains crossed-out text, transcribe only the final intended text.

- Preserve any underlining, circling, or emphasis if meaningful (use markdown formatting).
